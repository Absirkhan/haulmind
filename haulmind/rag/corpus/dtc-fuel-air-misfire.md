# Fuel, Air Intake and Misfire Codes
codes: P0087, P0101, P0300
system: engine

## P0087 — Fuel rail pressure too low
Fuel pressure under the commanded value. Causes: restricted fuel filter (most common),
weak lift pump, air in the fuel from a loose primary filter, or a failing high-pressure
pump. Symptoms: power loss on grades, hard starts. Medium severity: if the code is active
and power loss is reported, schedule a filter change and pressure test within 24–48 hours.

## P0101 — Mass air flow sensor range/performance
Sensor reading does not agree with calculated airflow. Dirty sensor element, boost leak,
or intake restriction. Low severity; clean or replace the sensor at the next service.
Watch for accompanying smoke or derate.

## P0300 — Random/multiple cylinder misfire
Multiple cylinders misfiring: injector issues, low fuel pressure (see P0087), or
compression loss. Medium severity. If paired with P0087, fix fuel supply first. Persistent
misfire with white smoke or coolant loss suggests a head gasket and warrants an urgent
inspection.

## Recommended action
Open a normal-priority work order when any of these codes stays active across two trips
or combines with a driver report of power loss. Fuel-system work typically runs 1–3 h.
