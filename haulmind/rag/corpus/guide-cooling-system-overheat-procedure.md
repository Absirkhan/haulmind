# Service Guide — Cooling System Overheat Procedure
codes: P0217, P0128, SPN110-FMI0, SPN110-FMI16, P0117
system: cooling

This guide is the fleet standard response when telematics shows coolant temperature
trending above 212 °F or any over-temperature code becomes active.

## Triage thresholds
| Coolant temperature | Trend | Response |
|---|---|---|
| 185–205 °F | stable | normal |
| 206–218 °F | rising for 30+ min | advise driver to reduce load; monitor |
| 219–229 °F | rising | high-priority work order; finish leg only if trend flattens |
| 230 °F or higher, or SPN 110 FMI 0 | any | stop at next safe location; urgent work order; recommend out-of-service |

A rise of more than 15 °F over six hours of steady driving is abnormal even if the
absolute value has not yet reached a threshold.

## Driver instructions
Reduce speed and avoid grades, turn the cab heater to maximum to shed heat, do not open
the radiator cap while hot, and report the gauge reading every 15 minutes until a shop is
assigned. If the engine protection system begins a shutdown countdown, pull over.

## Shop assignment
Prefer the nearest shop that lists **cooling** as a specialty and is open at the expected
arrival time. For an active over-temperature, distance beats rating: a 4.0-rated shop
20 miles away is better than a 4.6-rated shop 90 miles away.

## Work order content
Include the active codes, the coolant trend (start temperature, peak, hours), the unit's
odometer and whether P0128 preceded the event (thermostat suspect). Typical estimate:
thermostat 1.5 h, fan clutch 2–3 h, water pump 3–5 h; parts 200–1,400 USD.
