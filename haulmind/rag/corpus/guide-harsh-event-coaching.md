# Safety Guide — Harsh Event Patterns and Coaching
codes:
system: safety

## What counts
Telematics records harsh braking, harsh acceleration and harsh cornering when the
accelerometer exceeds about 0.35 g. Isolated events are normal: traffic, animals, a
cut-off. Fleet-wide, a driver averages roughly one harsh event per 20–30 driving hours.

## The pattern that matters
Four or more harsh events for one driver within 48 hours, spread across at least two
trips, is a **pattern**, not noise. Patterns correlate with fatigue, schedule pressure and
following too closely. Three events on one trip through a congested metro area is usually
traffic.

## Response
- Pattern detected: send a short, neutral coaching message (what was recorded, one
  concrete behaviour to change, when a coach will follow up). Tone matters: this is
  coaching, not discipline. Avoid blame language.
- Pattern plus speeding events or a harsh event above 0.55 g: schedule a ride-along or
  video review with the safety coach within the week.
- Never combine coaching with HOS or payroll topics in the same message.

## Message template
"Safety: {count} harsh braking/acceleration events were recorded in the last two days.
Please leave more following distance and smooth out braking; a coach will check in this
week. Reply if you had traffic or road conditions we should know about."
