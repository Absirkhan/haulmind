# P0520 and P0524 — Engine Oil Pressure
codes: P0520, P0524
system: lubrication

## P0524 — Engine oil pressure too low
A **critical** code. Oil pressure below the minimum for the current RPM means bearings are
at risk within minutes. Telematics oil pressure below 15 psi at road speed, or below 8 psi
at idle, confirms the fault. The driver must stop the engine at the first safe location.
Do not drive to a shop; arrange a tow. Recommend out-of-service immediately.

## P0520 — Oil pressure sensor circuit
A circuit code: the sensor or wiring is out of range. If telematics oil pressure reads a
flat implausible value (0 or 120 psi) while the engine runs normally, it is the sensor.
Medium severity: replace the sensor at normal priority, but do not ignore it because the
engine is now running without oil pressure protection.

## How to tell them apart
- P0524 with pressure trending down over minutes: real loss of pressure, stop the engine.
- P0520 with pressure frozen at a fixed value: sensor/circuit fault, normal priority.
- Both together: assume real low pressure until proven otherwise.
