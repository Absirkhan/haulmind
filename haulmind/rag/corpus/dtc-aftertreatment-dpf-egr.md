# Aftertreatment Codes — DPF, EGR and Catalyst Efficiency
codes: P2002, P2463, P0401, P0420
system: aftertreatment

## P2463 — DPF soot accumulation
Soot load in the diesel particulate filter is above the threshold for a passive or active
regeneration. Common after long periods of low-speed, low-load driving. First step is a
parked regeneration (30–45 minutes). If the code returns within a day of a successful
regen, suspect a faulty differential pressure sensor or excessive engine-out soot.

## P2002 — DPF efficiency below threshold
The filter is not trapping particulates as expected: cracked substrate, failed face seal,
or a differential pressure sensor reading low. Medium severity; schedule an aftertreatment
shop visit within a week. Continued operation risks fines but not immediate breakdown.

## P0401 — EGR flow insufficient
Exhaust gas recirculation valve stuck or passages carboned. Causes elevated NOx and may
trigger a derate after repeated occurrences. Low severity initially; schedule cleaning or
valve replacement at the next service if it recurs more than twice in a week.

## P0420 — Catalyst efficiency below threshold
Low severity on heavy-duty applications; often a lazy oxygen/NOx sensor rather than the
catalyst itself. Monitor; bundle with the next planned maintenance.

## Recommended action
None of these codes justify an urgent work order on their own. Use **normal** priority and
bundle them with other scheduled work. Escalate only if the ECM reports an active derate.
