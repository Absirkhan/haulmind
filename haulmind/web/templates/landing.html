WHAT IT CATCHES

The four things that go wrong on a long haul.

Drive-time limits

A driver will hit the 11-hour limit before the delivery. Haulmind finds a rested driver nearby and asks you to approve the handoff.

Engine trouble

A code keeps coming back and the coolant is creeping up. It reads the service guidance, finds a shop on the route, and drafts the work order.

Fuel-card misuse

A fill bigger than the tank, or miles from where the truck was. The purchase is held for review before anyone has to argue about it.

Unsafe driving

A pattern of hard braking across trips, not one bad corner. A calm coaching message goes out on its own, and you see it happened.
